# Timber

## Overview
Timber is used extensively in Sri Lankan construction for roof truss framing, formwork, scaffolding, doors, windows, and flooring.

## Common Species & Grades
- **Hardwoods**: Teak, Mahogany, Kumbuk, Jak, Nadun (furniture, doors, structural roof trusses).
- **Secondary / Softwoods**: Treated Rubberwood, Pine, Eucalyptus (formwork, ceiling joists, temporary shoring).

## Units & Measurement
- Measured in **linear meters (m)**, **cubic feet (sq.ft / cu.ft)**, or **pieces / planks**.
- Formwork plywood is sold in sheets (8ft x 4ft = 2.97 sqm).

## Reuse Considerations
- Reusable formwork timber and plywood sheets from finished sites are high-value surplus items.
- Ensure wood is free of protruding nails and stored flat under cover to prevent moisture warping.
