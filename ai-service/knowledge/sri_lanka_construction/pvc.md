# PVC Pipes

## Overview
Unplasticized Polyvinyl Chloride (uPVC) pipes are standard for cold water supply, drainage, rainwater downpipes, and electrical conduits in Sri Lankan buildings (e.g. S-lon, Anton, National PVC).

## Pressure Classes
- **Type 600 (Low Pressure)**: Rainwater gutters, drainage, electrical conduits.
- **Type 1000 (Medium Pressure)**: Domestic cold water distribution.
- **Type 1400 (High Pressure)**: Main pressure supply lines.

## Units & Lengths
- Standard pipe length: **4 meters** (or 6m for large main lines).
- Diameters: 20mm (1/2"), 25mm (3/4"), 32mm (1"), 50mm (2"), 110mm (4").

## Storage
- Protect uPVC pipes from prolonged direct sunlight exposure to prevent UV degradation and brittleness.
