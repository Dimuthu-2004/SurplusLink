# Construction Materials Overview

## Overview
Construction in Sri Lanka relies on standard categories of materials including cement, masonry blocks, aggregates, timber, steel rebar, tiles, paint, PVC piping, and waterproofing sealants.

## Common Material Categories
1. **Structural & Masonry**: Portland cement, concrete blocks, red clay bricks, river sand, metal aggregates (1/2", 3/4").
2. **Finishes**: Wall paint (emulsion, weather shield), floor tiles (ceramic, porcelain), wall tiles, wood stain, primer.
3. **Plumbing & Piping**: PVC pipes (Type 600, Type 1000), solvent cement, ball valves, fittings.
4. **Electrical & Fixtures**: Copper cabling, switches, circuit breakers, conduit pipes.
5. **Timber & Boards**: Teak, Mahogany, Treated Rubberwood, Plywood, MDF boards.
6. **Steel & Metals**: TMT Rebar (500W grade), GI pipes, box bars, structural steel channels.

## Storage & Climate Considerations
In Sri Lanka's tropical climate (high humidity and coastal salinity):
- Cement bags must be stored indoors off concrete floors on wooden pallets.
- Rebar must be elevated and protected from rain to prevent atmospheric oxidation.
- Timber must be seasoned and kept dry to prevent warp and fungal decay.
