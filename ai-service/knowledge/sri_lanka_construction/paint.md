# Paint

## Overview
Paints and protective coatings are essential finishing materials for interior walls, exterior facades, woodwork, and metal structures in Sri Lanka.

## Common Types
- **Interior Emulsion**: Water-based paint for interior walls and ceilings.
- **Exterior Weatherproof / Weather Shield**: Acrylic-based UV and rain resistant paint for exterior surfaces.
- **Enamel Paint**: Oil-based high gloss finish for timber doors, frames, and metalwork.
- **Undercoat / Primer**: Sealer applied prior to topcoat application.

## Typical Package Units
- Standard container sizes in Sri Lanka: **1 Litre**, **4 Litres** (1 Gallon container), **10 Litres**, **20 Litres** (Bucket).
- Specify cans/buckets and size (e.g. 2 cans of 4L = 8L total).

## Buyer & Seller Considerations
- **Colour & Preference**: Colour (e.g. Brilliant White, Off White, Yellow) is a key buyer preference.
- **Storage & Seal**: Cans must be tightly sealed and stored upright away from direct heat or sunlight. Unopened cans maintain quality for 2+ years.
- **Coverage**: 1 Litre of wall emulsion typically covers 10-12 square meters per coat.

## Reuse & Surplus
- Unopened leftover paint cans from completed commercial projects are ideal for surplus reuse.
