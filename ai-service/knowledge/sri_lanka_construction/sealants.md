# Sealants & Waterproofing

## Overview
Sealants, silicone caulks, polyurethane foam, and cementitious waterproofing slurries are applied to roofs, bathroom slabs, water tanks, and expansion joints in Sri Lankan structures.

## Typical Packaging
- **Silicone / PU Cartridges**: 280ml, 300ml, or 600ml sausage foils.
- **Waterproofing Slurry / Liquid Additives**: 1L, 5L, 20L cans, or 25kg powder bags (e.g. Sika, Fosroc, BASF).

## Expiry & Safety
- Waterproofing chemicals have strict expiry dates printed on containers. Sellers must verify expiry prior to listing.
