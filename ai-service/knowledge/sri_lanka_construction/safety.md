# Material Safety

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
When handling surplus materials during transport, loading, or unloading:
- **Safety Boots**: Steel-toed footwear for moving heavy aggregates, rebar, or timber.
- **Heavy Gloves**: Leather/rubber gloves when moving steel bars, glass, or cement bags.
- **Dust Masks**: Respirators for handling cement, lime, dry mortar, or cutting timber/tiles.
- **Eye Protection**: Safety goggles when inspecting chemicals, solvents, or machinery.

## Handling Heavy Loads
- Maximum manual lifting weight per worker: 25kg. Use two-person lift or machinery for 50kg cement bags or heavy equipment.
