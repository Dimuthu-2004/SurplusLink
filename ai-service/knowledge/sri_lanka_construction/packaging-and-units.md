# Packaging & Units

## Standard Units Matrix
- **Volume**: Litres (L), Millilitres (ml), Cubic Meters (m3).
- **Weight**: Kilograms (kg), Grams (g), Metric Tons (MT).
- **Area**: Square Meters (sqm / m2), Square Feet (sqft / ft2).
- **Length**: Meters (m), Millimeters (mm), Feet (ft).
- **Count**: Pieces (pcs), Units, Sets.

## Common Package Containers
- **Bags**: 50kg (Cement, Tile Adhesive, Mortar).
- **Cans / Buckets**: 1L, 4L, 10L, 20L (Paint, Primer, Sealers).
- **Boxes**: Tile boxes, Screw boxes, Fitting boxes.
- **Drums**: 200L (Bitumen, Formwork oil).
- **Cartridges**: 300ml (Silicone, Mastic).
