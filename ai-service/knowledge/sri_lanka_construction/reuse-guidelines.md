# Material Reuse Guidelines

## Principles of Circular Construction
1. **Reduce**: Minimize over-ordering through precise requirement planning.
2. **Reuse**: Transfer unconsumed, high-quality surplus materials from completed sites to new active projects.
3. **Recycle**: Reprocess damaged or broken brick/concrete into crushed aggregate base material.

## Material Condition Grading
- **NEW**: Unopened original packaging, recent manufacture date.
- **EXCELLENT**: Unopened or cleanly opened full containers, pristine condition.
- **GOOD**: Minor exterior package cosmetic wear, material 100% sound.
- **FAIR**: Partially used open batch or minor surface oxidation, functional for non-critical work.
- **POOR**: Heavily degraded material suitable only for landfill or scrap recovery.
