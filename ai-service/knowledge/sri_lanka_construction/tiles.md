# Tiles

## Overview
Ceramic and porcelain tiles are widely used for flooring, bathroom walls, and outdoor patios in Sri Lanka.

## Typical Dimensions & Packaging
- Common sizes: 300x300mm, 600x600mm, 600x1200mm, 300x600mm.
- Sold in **boxes** containing a specific coverage area in square meters (sqm) or piece count per box.
- Example: 600x600mm tiles often come in boxes of 4 pieces = 1.44 sqm per box.

## Buyer & Seller Considerations
- **Shade & Batch Match**: Tiles from different manufacturing batches may have slight color shade differences. Surplus listings should state batch numbers where available.
- **Wastage Margin**: Buyers should order 5-10% extra area to allow for cutting and fitting.

## Storage
- Store tile boxes vertically on edge on dry pallets to prevent cracked tiles.
