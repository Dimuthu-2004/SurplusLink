# Storage & Preservation

## Weather Protection in Sri Lanka
- Sri Lanka experiences heavy monsoon downpours and high humidity.
- **Cement**: Store indoors on wooden pallets, sealed from moist air.
- **Paint**: Keep in cool, shaded areas below 30°C. Never freeze or overheat.
- **Timber**: Stack horizontally with spacers (stickers) to allow air circulation.
- **Rebar**: Elevate above rain puddles; cover with breathable tarpaulins.
- **Tiles**: Keep dry to avoid carton deterioration and tile breakage.
