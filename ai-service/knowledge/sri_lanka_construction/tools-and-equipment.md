# Tools & Equipment

## Overview
Construction sites use portable power tools, hand tools, light machinery, and heavy equipment.

## Common Categories
- **Power Tools**: Angle grinders, rotary hammer drills, circular saws, concrete poker vibrators.
- **Machinery**: Diesel generators (5kVA - 50kVA), air compressors, concrete mixers, plate compactors.
- **Hand Tools**: Trowels, spirit levels, measuring tapes, bolt cutters, wheelbarrows.
- **Temporary Works & Scaffolding**: Steel props, jacks, H-frame scaffolding, safety nets.

## Unit Mode & Listing
- Listed under **PIECE** quantity mode (e.g. 1 Generator, 2 Compressors).
- Sellers specify operational status, fuel type (Diesel/Petrol/Electric), and power rating.
