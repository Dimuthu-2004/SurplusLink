# Steel

## Overview
Steel reinforcement bars (rebar) and structural steel sections form the backbone of reinforced concrete construction in Sri Lanka.

## Common Types
- **Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) Rebar**: High yield strength steel (Grade 500W). Standard bar diameters: 10mm, 12mm, 16mm, 20mm, 25mm.
- **Structural Steel**: GI box bars, C-channels, I-beams, angle irons, steel wire mesh.

## Units & Packaging
- Standard bar length: **6 meters** or **12 meters**.
- Sold by **metric ton (MT)**, **kilograms (kg)**, or **full bar pieces**.

## Storage & Safety
- Keep rebar off bare wet earth using timber bolsters.
- Surface light flash rust is acceptable if structural diameter is uncompromised, but heavy scaling rust requires scrubbing or rejection.
