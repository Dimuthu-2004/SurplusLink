# Aggregates & Sand

## Overview
Aggregates and sand constitute over 70% of concrete volume. In Sri Lanka, manufactured sand (M-sand) and river sand are used alongside crushed granite metal.

## Common Grades
- **River Sand / M-Sand**: Fine aggregates for mortar, plastering, and concrete mix.
- **1/2" (12.5mm) Metal**: Coarse aggregate for thin slab casting and precast elements.
- **3/4" (20mm) Metal**: Standard coarse aggregate for structural concrete foundations, beams, and columns.
- **Quarry Dust**: Base material for paving blocks and land filling.

## Units & Transport
- Measured in **Cubic Meters (m3)** or **Cubes** (1 Cube = 100 cubic feet = approx 2.83 m3).
- Delivered by tipper trucks.
