# Sustainability in Sri Lanka Construction

## Environmental Impact
Construction activities account for approximately 35% of industrial waste in Sri Lanka. Diverting unused surplus materials from landfills prevents embodied carbon loss and conserves virgin natural resources (e.g. river sand, timber, limestone).

## Green Building Rating Points
Using surplus and salvaged materials earns GREEN SL rating points under Material & Resources credits:
- **MR Credit 2**: Construction Waste Management & Diversion.
- **MR Credit 3**: Building Material Reuse.
- **MR Credit 4**: Local Material Sourcing (reducing long-distance transport emissions).
