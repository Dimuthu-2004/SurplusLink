# Cement (සිමෙන්ති / Simenthi / ගබඩා)

## Overview
Cement is the fundamental hydraulic binder used in Sri Lankan masonry, concrete works, and plastering. Standard bags in Sri Lanka are distributed in 50kg bags (e.g. Tokyo Super, Sanstha, Nippon Cement).

## Common Types
- **Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC)**: High early strength, ideal for structural columns, beams, and precast concrete.
- **Portland Composite Cement (PCC)**: Blended with fly ash or slag, widely used for general masonry, block laying, and plastering.

## Typical Packaging & Units
- Standard unit: **50kg bags** or metric tons for bulk delivery.
- When buying or selling on SurplusLink, cement is specified in **bags** (e.g., 7 bags of 50kg = 350kg total).

## Buyer & Seller Considerations
- **Condition Check**: Cement bags must be sealed, free of hard lumps, and dry.
- **Shelf Life**: Unopened cement stored under dry conditions has a recommended shelf life of 3 months from manufacture date.

## Storage
- Store in a dry, enclosed shed.
- Keep bags at least 15cm off the floor on raised wooden pallets.
- Stack no more than 10 bags high to prevent compaction and bursting.

## Safety Notes
- Cement dust causes skin irritation and eye injuries. Use protective gloves and dust masks when handling.
