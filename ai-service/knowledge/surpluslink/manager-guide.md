# Manager Guide

## Overview
Managers supervise the SurplusLink ecosystem, maintaining catalog integrity, approving seller listings, validating multi-seller match allocations, and monitoring overall marketplace health.

## Key Responsibilities
1. **Catalog & Template Management**:
   - Manage material categories and construction item templates.
   - Define custom seller attribute schemas (e.g. thickness, fuel type, colour).
2. **Listing Approvals**:
   - Review pending seller listings for authenticity, correct packaging specification, and fair market pricing.
   - Approve or reject seller listings with notes.
3. **Requirement Match Approvals**:
   - Review match recommendations generated by the AI matching engine for buyer requirements.
   - Verify multi-seller allocations, total fulfillment, logistics distance, and total transaction value.
   - Approve match groups to create official buyer/seller offers and reserve inventory.
4. **Transaction Supervision**:
   - Resolve disputes or non-delivery issues when handovers require manager review.
   - Finalize or release allocations if timelines expire.
