# Buyer Guide

## Overview
Buyers use SurplusLink to source construction materials, tools, and equipment efficiently across Sri Lanka.

## Creating a Requirement
- Buyers can browse available materials on the marketplace or submit a requirement.
- When submitting a requirement, buyers specify:
  1. Material Item (e.g. Paint, Cement, Floor Tiles, Rebar).
  2. Required Quantity & Unit (e.g. 10 L, 50 bags, 100 sqm).
  3. Maximum Budget (in LKR).
  4. Preferred Condition (New, Excellent, Good, Fair).
  5. Preferences (e.g. Colour: Yellow, Brand: Tokyo Super).
  6. Delivery Location (City / District in Sri Lanka).
  7. Need-by Date / Deadline.

## Requirement Drafts & AI Assistant
- The SurplusLink AI Assistant helps buyers create requirement drafts from natural language in English, Sinhala, Tamil, or Romanized Sinhala (e.g., "mata yellow paint 10L one Negombo walata").
- The AI creates a **Requirement Draft** for buyer review.
- The buyer must explicitly review and submit the requirement. The AI never submits requirements automatically.

## Matching & Approval
- Once submitted, the AI Matching Engine finds single or multi-seller combinations to fulfill the requirement.
- The manager reviews recommended match groups.
- Upon manager approval, offers are created and inventory is reserved.

## Receiving Materials
- When the seller hands over materials, the buyer inspects them and confirms receipt in the mobile app.
- Once confirmed, the transaction completes.
