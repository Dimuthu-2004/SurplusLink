# Safety & Platform Policies

## Listing Standards
- All listings must represent genuine surplus or unused construction stock, tools, or equipment.
- Hazardous chemical waste, expired structural chemicals, or prohibited substances are strictly banned.
- Sellers must accurately report condition (New, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).

## Privacy & Security
- Private contact details (full names, phone numbers, exact addresses) are disclosed only between matched transaction partners after manager approval.
- AI Assistant never discloses private records of other users.

## Quality & Verification
- Buyers are advised to inspect materials upon pickup/delivery before confirming receipt.
- Structural elements (e.g. TMT rebar, structural timber) must meet relevant Sri Lanka Standards (SLS) for reuse safety.
