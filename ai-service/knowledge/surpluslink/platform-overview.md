# SurplusLink Platform Overview

## Overview
SurplusLink is Sri Lanka's dedicated construction material reuse and surplus marketplace. The platform connects construction contractors, suppliers, and individuals who have excess construction materials, tools, or equipment with buyers needing materials for construction projects.

## Key Goals
- Reduce construction waste across Sri Lankan building sites.
- Provide affordable quality materials to buyers.
- Enable sellers to monetize unused surplus inventory.
- Automate multi-seller matching and logistics using AI optimization.

## Key Roles
- **Buyer**: Requests construction materials by submitting specific project requirements (quantity, budget, preferences, location).
- **Seller**: Lists verified surplus inventory with specific packaging modes, units, pricing, and locations.
- **Manager**: Oversees marketplace integrity, approves seller listings, approves requirement matches, and monitors transactions.

## Core Features
- **Dynamic Construction Catalog**: Supports materials, tools, equipment, fixtures, and temporary works with template-driven attributes.
- **Universal Quantity Model**: Handles base units (kg, L, sqm, m3, pcs) and physical package containers (cans, bags, boxes, drums) cleanly.
- **AI Matching Engine**: Evaluates item identity, quantity availability, material cost, logistics distance, transport cost, item condition, and buyer preference compatibility.
- **Grouped Manager Approval**: Multi-seller requirement matches are batched for manager verification before stock reservation.
- **Handover & Receipt Transaction Flow**: Secure milestone-based transaction lifecycle with OTP and verified receipts.
